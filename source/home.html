How technical SEO is scoped

Inside a technical recommendation

  1. The affected URL or template

    The URL or shared template where the issue was found.

  2. The observation and evidence

    The test performed and the evidence supporting the finding.

  3. The proposed change and its owner

    The recommended correction and the person responsible for implementing it.

  4. The implementation check

    The published change, the verification check and any unresolved items.

Questions about
working with us.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Your website URL, the country or audience you want to reach, and the problem or assignment you have in mind. Do not include account passwords.

Can you make the changes as well as recommend them?

Implementation can be included where the platform and access allow it. The proposal distinguishes changes we handle from work assigned to your developer or team.

What will the report show?

A record of completed tasks and affected pages, with performance observations from the agreed data sources. Tracking gaps and unresolved work are identified alongside the next recommendations.

Do you guarantee rankings or revenue?

No. The agreement defines the SEO work. Search positions, traffic and commercial outcomes are influenced by factors beyond any one assignment.
