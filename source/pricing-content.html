Read the proposal against these questions.

What will be delivered?

Identify the named website areas and outputs. Establish whether a task ends with recommendations, published changes or a follow-up implementation check.

Who needs to contribute?

Record access requirements, development dependencies and the factual information your organisation must supply. Agree the contact responsible for reviewing changes.

What sits outside the fee?

Check how additional development, software and other third-party costs are handled. Extra work should be agreed before it is carried out.

How is the engagement reviewed?

Confirm reporting dates, revision arrangements and the process for changing or ending the assignment. Your proposal is the reference for those terms.
